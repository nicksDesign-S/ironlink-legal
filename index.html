IronLink is developed and operated by Nick Seymore, an individual developer ("we," "us," "our"). You can reach us at nicholasseymore21@gmail.com with any privacy questions or requests.

Who can use IronLink

IronLink is for adults only. You must be 18 years of age or older to create an account. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and accounts found to belong to someone under 18 will be removed.

Information we collect

Account information: your email address and password (handled securely by our authentication provider, Supabase, and never stored in plain text).

Profile information you provide: first name, age, gender, gym(s), workout split, experience level, training goals, favorite lift, bio, lift stats (bench/squat/deadlift), and your weekly availability.

Photos and videos you upload: profile photos and PR (personal record) videos you choose to share.

Activity data: who you swipe on, lift requests you send or receive, the gym partners and groups you match and chat with, and the messages you send within the app.

We only collect what the app needs to function — we don't collect your precise location, contacts, or anything outside of what's listed above.

How we use your information

To operate core app features: matching you with compatible gym partners, lift requests, chats, and your profile.

To send you account-related emails, such as password reset codes.

To keep the app safe (for example, responding to reports of inappropriate content or behavior).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share information with

We use a small number of service providers to run IronLink, and they only receive what's needed to provide their service:

Supabase — our backend provider, for authentication, database storage, and file storage (photos/videos).

Expo / EAS — for building and distributing the app.

Resend — for delivering transactional emails (like password reset codes). Resend only receives your email address and the content of that specific email.

Other IronLink users can see the profile information, photos, and videos you choose to make part of your profile, along with anything you send in chats or lift requests.

Your choices and rights

Edit your profile at any time from the Profile tab.

Delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, photos, videos, and data. You may also email us at nicholasseymore21@gmail.com to request deletion.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your photos, videos, and profile details as described above.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including relying on Supabase's security practices for authentication and storage. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
